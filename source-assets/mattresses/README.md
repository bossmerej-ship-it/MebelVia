# Mattress selection illustrations

Updated 2026-11-01 using the built-in ImageGen tool. These are generated, unbranded
selection illustrations, not photographs of confirmed supplier models. The owner
confirmed that all three entries should remain examples for selection. Do not use
their visible construction as evidence of a supplier's actual specifications.

The selected outputs live in `dist/assets/mattresses/`:

| Example | Card export | Large export |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | `spring-mattress-studio-576.webp` | `spring-mattress-studio-1152.webp` |
| Foam | `foam-mattress-studio-576.webp` | `foam-mattress-studio-1152.webp` |
| Topper | `topper-studio-576.webp` | `topper-studio-1152.webp` |

All images use the same 4:3 canvas. Original tool outputs were 1448×1086 PNGs;
Sharp exported 576×432 and 1152×864 WebP at quality 85 without cropping,
retouching, sharpening or changing their composition. The small set totals
32,712 bytes; the large set totals 159,322 bytes. Only the required candidate is
requested by the browser; cards remain lazy-loaded and dialog images load immediately.

## Final prompts

### Spring

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: ONE realistic catalog product photograph for a light furniture shop, landscape 4:3 composition. Subject: a generic unbranded double-size pocket-spring mattress, about 20cm deep, with an ivory-white jacquard knitted ticking top, shallow softly quilted elongated loops (not inflated identical pillows), fine genuine textile weave, subtle wrinkles where cloth meets piping. Warm light-grey woven side panel with restrained horizontal quilting and two stitched fabric carrying handles on the visible long side. Real piping follows the softly square corners. Fully assembled intact mattress, no cutaway, no floating layers. Camera a modest elevated three-quarter angle from the foot-left corner with the foot face and right long side visible, believable rectangular 160x200 proportions, entire mattress fully visible including shadow. The long diagonal fills about 87% of image width, centered, 8-10% safe space all sides. Seamless PURE WHITE #ffffff studio background blending into a white website, no horizon line or beige panel. Soft daylight studio product photography, restrained contact shadow, balanced white exposure preserving actual fabric detail, tack-sharp natural lens rendering. Make the mattress convincingly manufactured and tactile, neither inflated nor polished. No branding, text, logo, tag, watermark, room, bedframe, props, synthetic smoothness, plastic, excessive tufting, shiny edges, exaggerated bubbles. This is a generic selection example, not an image of a branded model.
